# User guide (donors and requesters)

DonorConnect AI helps people who need blood find willing donors, **without sharing anyone's contact details unless the donor agrees**. It does not give medical advice and
never decides whether you may donate: eligibility is recorded by an authorised blood service.

## Getting started
1. **Register** as a *donor* (pick your blood group and city) or as a *requester*. You must confirm you are 18 or older. Confirm your e-mail if asked, then sign in.
2. Complete **Profile**. Donors can switch **Available** on/off at any time.
3. **Alerts** shows notifications live. In *Notification settings* you may opt in to generic e-mails (they only say "you have a new notification" and link back).
4. The **Assistant** answers how-to questions. In an emergency call your local emergency number; the assistant will not give medical advice.

## Donors
* **Opportunities**: approved requests that are preliminarily compatible with your blood group (information only; clinicians confirm real compatibility). Filter by city or group.
* **Accept** a request. You choose whether to **share your contact details** with that requester; you can change this later in **My offers** (revoking hides your details again immediately). Withdrawing an offer also hides them.
* **Invitations** arrive in **My offers**; you can accept or decline.
* **My activity** shows your own history: invitations, accepted, withdrawn and completed offers (an accepted offer on a request later marked fulfilled), reply times, and a timeline. You can download your own offers as CSV. Nobody else's data is shown.

## Requesters
* **New request**: blood group, units, city, hospital, urgency, needed-by date. Use an **alias** for the patient, never a real name or medical record details.
* A new request is **reviewed by an administrator** before donors can see it. You are notified of the decision (with a reason if rejected).
* **My requests** shows status and, once donors accept, how many have accepted; contact details appear only for donors who consented. Mark the request **Fulfilled** (or cancel) when done.
* **Insights** shows your own request statistics: status mix, approval, response and fulfilment times, and a CSV of your requests.

## Reading the numbers
"—" means there is nothing to divide by (for example no reviewed requests yet), not zero. Times use only requests that reached both steps; the page states how many. All times are UTC.

## Your privacy
Your phone/e-mail are shown to a requester only for offers where you ticked consent. Reports and exports never include names, phone numbers, e-mails, hospitals or patient aliases of other people.
Install as an app (PWA) is optional; the app never stores personal pages offline.
